| **Interviewer** | Malak Alfakavi (team lead) |

## Questions and answers

**1. How many certificates does your club issue per semester or per event?**

> "Roughly 150 to 200 certificates per semester, and at each workshop or large event we hand out between 50 and 70 certificates to attendees."

**2. If a company asks you whether a certificate is real, how do you check it and how long does it take?**

> "Right now we search manually through the Excel files and the Google Drive folder linked to the event to confirm the person's name. It takes about one to two days before we can reply to them."

**3. Can we test the system with your club in December?**

> "Yes, of course! We will have workshops in December before the finals, and we can use your system to issue the certificates to the students and measure the experience."

## What this means for the project

- **Scale:** 150–200 certificates per semester; batches of 50–70 per event → the dashboard must issue a whole event in one batch (story S3).
- **Current verification:** manual search in Excel / Google Drive, 1–2 days → baseline for objective O1 (target: under 10 seconds).
- **Trial:** agreed for the club's December workshops → milestone on 7 Dec 2026.
